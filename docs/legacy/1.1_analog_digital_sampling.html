4 · Aliasing & the Nyquist limit

A pure sine wave (blue) is sampled N times across the window (green dots). If you don't sample fast enough, those same dots also fit a slower wave (orange dashed) — the signal is aliased. Nyquist: you need at least 2 samples per cycle.
